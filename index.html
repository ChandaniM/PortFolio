About

I'm a software engineer with of experience, mostly working in frontend development. I'm from Mumbai, a city that never sleeps. There's always traffic, noise, people rushing somewhere, and a little chaos everywhere. Somehow, in the middle of all that, you learn to keep moving. My journey in tech has been a little like that too.

I've always loved sketching. I enjoy taking something that exists in my head and turning it into something I can actually see. That's what naturally pulled me toward frontend development. I loved taking an idea, turning it into an interface, and seeing a real person interact with it. Over the years, I've worked with Angular, React, and Svelte, along with responsive design, performance, testing, and UX.

But I've always been curious about what happens beyond the screen. I started exploring backend development with Node.js and Express, learned about SQL and NoSQL databases, and eventually found myself getting interested in DevOps. I started asking questions like, “How does this application actually reach the user?” and “What happens when the traffic increases or a server goes down?” That curiosity made me want to understand the whole journey of an application, not just the part I could see.

I've also worked as a freelancer, where I get to start with someone's idea, understand what they actually need, create a demo, and then build the product with them step by step. That experience taught me that development is not just about writing code. It's also about listening, understanding, communicating, and turning someone's idea into something real.

Frontend is still where I feel most at home, but I don't want to limit myself to just one part of technology. I enjoy building things, exploring how they work, and learning what happens behind the scenes.

I think that curiosity is what brought me into tech, and it's probably what will keep me exploring it for a long time.

Feel free to reach out on LinkedIn. Always happy to talk code, solve interesting problems, learn something new, or just swap notes over coffee.
